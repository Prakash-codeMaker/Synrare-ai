## [00:00 – 00:30] HOOK — THE PROBLEM

**[Show: blank/dark screen, then title card]**

"There are over 7,000 rare diseases in the world.
And for most of them... doctors see fewer than 50 patients in their entire career.

AI needs data to learn. Thousands of examples, ideally tens of thousands.
But rare diseases have almost none.

Today we're showing you SynRareAI —
a 6-agent AI system that solves this with synthetic data and zero privacy risk."

## [00:30 – 01:00] THE NUMBERS — BEFORE

**[Show: Setup tab, accuracy bar showing 61.3%]**

"A standard diagnostic model trained on 47 rare disease patient records
gets 61.3% accuracy. That's barely better than guessing.

And collecting more real data? That takes 3 to 5 years — if you can even find
enough patients with privacy consent.

This is the rare disease data wall.
SynRareAI breaks it."

## [01:00 – 01:45] SELECT + UPLOAD

**[Action: Click 'Gaucher's Disease']**

"Let's pick Gaucher's Disease — a metabolic disorder affecting 1 in 40,000 people.

We've uploaded 47 anonymised patient records — 12 clinical features each.

**[Action: Click upload area → data preview appears]**

"Now — let's run the pipeline."

**[Action: Click '⚡ RUN AI PIPELINE' button]**

---

## [01:45 – 02:30] PIPELINE — AGENTS IN ACTION

**[Show: Pipeline tab, agents lighting up one by one]**

"The Orchestrator routes first to the SynData Agent —
generating 2,500 privacy-safe synthetic patient records
from our 47 real ones. Using a GaussianCopula synthesiser.

Watch the agent status matrix — each agent activates in sequence."

**[Show: Privacy Guard activating]**

"Now the Privacy Guard applies differential privacy —
epsilon 0.3, delta 1e-5.

It then runs a membership inference attack — simulating a real adversary
trying to identify if any real patient's data was used.

The attack gets 51.2% accuracy. That's a coin flip.
Your patients' data is mathematically protected."

**[Show: Classifier activating]**

"The Classifier trains first on real data — 61.3%.
Then retrains with real plus synthetic — 84.7%."

---

## [02:30 – 03:15] RESULTS DASHBOARD

**[Show: Results tab auto-appears]**

"And here are the results.

Look at this chart — baseline versus augmented.

The training curve confirms it — synthetic augmentation lifts performance

And the privacy compliance panel — every check green.

**[Show: Synthetic image grid + clinical report]**

"12 of the 2,500 synthetic patient samples — rendered here.
